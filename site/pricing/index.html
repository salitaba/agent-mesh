Pricing

Pricing for the runtime.

You pay for the licensed limits and the support that comes with them. The model is billed to you by your own provider, at your own rates, and we never touch it. Prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes.

There are two ways to pay: a flat monthly plan where we run it for you, or a licence for the software you run yourself. Either way the model is billed to you by your own provider, at your own rates, and we never touch it. Prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes.

What you are paying for

Licensed limits, and the support that comes with them.

A plan is a licence to run the runtime up to a size, plus the help that goes with it. It is not a price for the model, and it is not a lock.

Licensed limits

How many projects may be open at once, how many agents a mesh may have, and how many agent turns may run at the same moment. The Community plan’s limits are what the source licence lets anyone run in production for free; a paid plan is the licence to run beyond them.

Features

Usage export (JSON and CSV) and Prometheus metrics, from the Team plan. The event log that the usage report is computed from is yours on every plan.

Support

The Community plan is supported through the issue tracker and the documentation. Team and Business add email support with the response targets in the table, and Business adds support for the container image and Helm chart deployment. Enterprise has a named contact and a support agreement.

What you are not paying for

  • Model usage.The agents run on credentials that belong to you, and your provider bills you for them, with your own discounts and rate limits. We do not resell it and we do not add a margin to it.
  • A hosted service.Curule is software you run, on your own infrastructure. We cannot see, start or stop anything on it.

How licences work

A key by email, checked on your machine.

There is no checkout, no account and no billing system on this site. A paid plan starts with an email, and the licence key is issued by hand.

A licence for software you run starts with an email, and the key is issued by hand: this site has no checkout or billing for it. Curule Cloud has its own sign-up and billing.

  1. Write to us

    Say which plan, how many projects and agents you run, and the name the licence should carry.

  2. We issue a key by hand

    A licence is one line of text, signed with a key that stays with us. We send it to you by email.

  3. Install it on the host

    curule license install <key>

    Or put it in MESH_LICENSE in the environment. A running host reads it again within 30 seconds, so there is nothing to restart.

  4. It is checked offline

    The signature is verified on your machine. Nothing phones home, and an air-gapped install verifies a licence exactly as a connected one does.

  5. When it expires

    You are told 30 days ahead. After the date the plan’s limits stay in force for a grace period (14 days by default), and then the instance is on the Community plan. Nothing is deleted and nothing that is running is stopped; install a renewed key and the plan returns.

Limits are reported, not enforced, by default. MESH_LICENSE_ENFORCEMENT is warn unless you change it: an instance over its plan’s limits says so in the log, the dashboard, the API and the metrics, and keeps running. With enforce, a mesh over its seat limit will not start and a project over the open-project cap will not open; what is already running is never stopped. The licence terms, not a lock in the code, are what bind a customer to a plan.

Questions

Pricing questions.

Is the price per agent?

No. A price is flat for a plan. The plan sets how many projects may be open, how many agents one mesh may have and how many agent turns may run at the same moment.

Does the price include the model?

No. The agents run on model credentials you own (any OpenAI-compatible provider, the Anthropic API, Bedrock, Vertex or Foundry), and your provider bills you for the model. The calculator above shows the two figures separately, and the model figure is an estimate from one measured mission.

What happens if we go over a limit?

By default you are told and nothing is refused. Running beyond the Community plan’s limits in production is what a paid plan licenses; without one it is outside the source licence, whatever the runtime lets you do.

Can the price change under us?

A licence carries its plan, its limits and its expiry, so a change to the list price takes effect at renewal, when a new licence is issued.

How do we buy?

Write to us: see how licences work above. There is no checkout, and we agree the terms with you before we issue a key.

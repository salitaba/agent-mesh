Contact

Write to us.

What to put in the message

To sales

Please say

  • which plan you are asking about;
  • how many projects you would have open, and the most agents in one team;
  • whether you would pay monthly or annually;
  • the name the licence should carry;
  • whether the install is air-gapped.

It goes to a person, not to a form. If we agree, a licence key is issued by hand.

To support

Please include

  • the plan you are on;
  • the output of curule doctor, which is safe to paste into a ticket;
  • what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • how you run it: Docker, Compose or Helm.

Response times follow your plan; they are in the comparison table. The Community plan is supported through the issue tracker and the documentation: open an issue.

To security

Please include

  • what you found;
  • how to reproduce it: a request, a configuration, a version;
  • what an attacker gains;
  • whether you have told anyone else.

Do not open a public issue. We acknowledge a report within 3 business days. What to expect, and the policy.

What we can help with

So that you do not write to the wrong place, or wait for something we cannot do.

  • We answerQuestions about plans, licences and pricing; help with installing and running Curule, as your plan provides; and security reports.
  • We cannot see your instanceNothing phones home, so we know only what you send us. That is also why we ask for the output of curule doctor.
  • We do not run it for youIt is software you run on your own infrastructure. It is not offered as a hosted service.
  • Curule Cloud has its own sign-upYou do not need to write to us to start: create an account and choose a plan. A licence is for the software you run on your own infrastructure.
  • Your model provider answers the restQuestions about your model provider, its prices, limits and outages, go to it.

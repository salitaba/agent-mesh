before this passafter
  • A name is content, not a label. The roster and the canvas now spend their width on the seat's name before anything else; role, flags and wire counts are subordinate lines. At 360 the roster name gets 194 px and no longer cuts; at 1440 a long name wraps to two lines instead of reading frontend-….
  • A thing that covers the page is a layer. The inspector drawer, the YAML preview and the assistant dock now have a scrim, a named modal dialog, a held page, a Tab trap, a 44 px way out and focus returned to the opener. Before, the phone inspector had none of these while covering all 360 × 844 px.
  • One move is open at a time. The first-run guide was four open instructions (553 px) above a roster that started at y=881 on an 844 px screen. It now opens only the step that is *now*, states Step 1 of 4, and the first seat row begins at y=795 — on the screen.
  • One voice. Every Designer state uses the same lede; the drawer's heading says what it is editing (Seat: pm, Mesh settings) rather than Inspector; a checks error with no field now names the route out instead of ending at "edit mesh.yaml".

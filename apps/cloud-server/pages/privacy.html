What we keep about you

  • Your email address and a salted scrypt hash of your password. We cannot read your password.
  • Your sessions: when each began and was last used, and the address and browser it came from, so that you and we can see them. A session ends 30 days after it began or after 14 days without use.
  • Billing: your plan, the payment provider's reference for you, and the reference and amount of each payment. Card details are handled by the payment provider and do not reach us.
  • Usage: for each call a workspace's agents make to a model, the time, the model, the token counts, what it cost and what you were charged. The ledger that holds this does not store the text of what was sent or returned.
  • Your workspace's contents: its projects, event log, artifacts and files, in the workspace's own storage. Deleting the workspace deletes them.

Who else receives it

  • The model providers that answer your agents' calls receive the text of those calls, which is how they answer.
  • The payment provider receives what it needs to take a payment.
  • A mail service delivers the links we send you.

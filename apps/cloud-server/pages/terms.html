What the service is

Curule Cloud runs Curule workspaces on infrastructure the operator controls, and supplies the models they call. A workspace is one isolated host with its own projects, event log and files. The software in a workspace is the same Curule that can be run on your own machines.

Plans, credit and payment

  • A plan renews each period until it is cancelled, and includes an amount of model usage that is replaced, not added to, each period.
  • Credit you add does not expire and is spent after a plan's included usage. When the balance is zero, calls to models are refused.
  • If a payment fails, workspaces keep running for 3 days and are then stopped, not deleted. If a subscription ends, workspaces stop, and are deleted 30 days later.
  • Deleting a workspace deletes its data. That cannot be undone.

Payments are taken by Stripe, on Stripe's own page, so card details do not reach us.

Refunds and cancellation. You can cancel a plan at any time from your account; it then ends at the close of the period you paid for. You can ask for a refund of a payment within 14 days of making it, by writing to ali79taba@gmail.com.

Currency and tax. Prices are in US dollars. Where the law requires tax to be charged, it is added to the price and shown before you pay.

Price changes. If a price changes, we write to you by email at least 30 days before the new price applies to you.

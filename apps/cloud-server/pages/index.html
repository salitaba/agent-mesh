Plans

02

What you get

  • A workspace of your ownOne isolated host with its own projects, event log and files. Deleting a workspace deletes everything in it.
  • Model usage, statedEach plan says whether it includes model usage or you bring a key, so the price on this page is the price you pay.
  • Your own model keyYour agents call the model provider you choose, with a key you give your workspace and nobody can read back. Anthropic, or any provider that speaks the OpenAI chat format: OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Gemini's compatible endpoint.
  • Models includedEach plan includes an amount of model usage at the service's rates. When it runs out, you can add credit at any time.
  • Hosting, not tokensCurule does not resell model usage and keeps no balance for it. What a team uses is billed to you by your provider, on your own account, at your provider's prices.
  • Included usage, then creditHosting and model usage are on one bill. Credit you add does not expire, and it stays with the account when a plan's included amount is spent.
  • Your record is yoursIt is the same append-only event log a self-hosted install writes, so a mission can be replayed and its record read anywhere.
03

How it works

  1. Create your accountAn email and a password. We send a link to confirm your address. Nothing is charged yet.
  2. Choose a planOne flat price for each period, paid on the payment provider's page. The card says what it includes and how many workspaces your account may run at once.
  3. Make a workspaceOne click starts your own Curule host on our servers. The plan says what it includes. You give it your model key once it has started. Its plan's model usage is ready to use.
  4. Describe your teamOpen the workspace in your browser, describe the team you want, and start a mission. Every action is recorded.
04

How billing works

  • Each periodOne payment covers one period: the servers your workspaces run on, their storage, and what the plan includes. The plan sets how many workspaces your account may run at once.
  • Model usageThe plan says whether model usage is included in the price or billed to you by your provider.
  • Model usageIt is not part of the payment. Your model provider bills you for it directly.
  • Model usageThe plan includes an amount each period. When it runs out, add credit from your balance and keep going.
  • If a payment failsYour workspaces keep running for 3 days and are then stopped, not deleted. A payment that arrives before then puts everything back.
  • If you cancelYour workspaces stop, and they are deleted 30 days later.

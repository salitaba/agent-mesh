Run a mesh without running a server.

Curule Cloud is the runtime, run for you. Sign up, pick a plan and open a workspace in your browser: the same dashboard and the same agents as the self-hosted product, on servers we operate. You install nothing and rent nothing. You bring your own model key, and you pay your model provider directly.

How it works

  1. Sign upAn email and a password. We send a link to confirm your address.
  2. Pick a planA flat price for hosting your workspace, paid on the payment provider's page.
  3. Make a workspaceOne click starts your own Curule host. You give it your model key once it has started.One click starts your own Curule host, with the service's models ready to use. Each plan lists the usage it includes.
  4. Describe your team and startOpen the workspace in your browser, describe the team you want, and start a mission.
  • A workspace of your ownOne isolated host with its own projects, event log and files. Deleting a workspace deletes them.
  • Your own model keyYour agents call the model provider you choose, with a key you give your workspace and nobody can read back. Anthropic, or any provider that speaks the OpenAI chat format: OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Gemini's compatible endpoint.
  • Hosting, not tokensCurule does not resell model usage and keeps no balance for it. What a team uses is billed to you by your provider, on your own account, at your provider's prices.
  • Your record is yoursIt is the same append-only event log a self-hosted install writes, so a mission can be replayed and its record read anywhere.

Plans

How billing works

  • Each periodA plan's payment is for hosting your workspace for one period: its servers, its storage and the limits of the plan.
  • Model usageIt is not part of the payment. Your model provider bills you for it directly.
  • If a payment failsYour workspaces keep running for 3 days and are then stopped, not deleted. A payment that arrives before then puts everything back.
  • If you cancelYour workspaces stop, and they are deleted 30 days later.
